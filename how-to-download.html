How to Download YouTube Videos & MP3s

Published: November 2026 • 5 min read • Compatible with all operating systems

Whether you're preparing for a long flight, saving tutorial videos for offline coding sessions, or compiling an offline road trip playlist, YTmate makes downloading YouTube media effortless. Follow this detailed tutorial for your specific device.

1. Downloading on PC or Mac (Windows, macOS, Linux)

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, Brave, or Opera).
  2. Go to YouTube and navigate to the video you want to download.
  3. Click the URL bar at the top of the browser and copy the address (or right-click the video and select "Copy video URL").
  4. Navigate to YTmate.online.
  5. Paste the link into the search input and click Download Now.
  6. Choose your desired format:
    • For video: select 1080p Full HD, 4K, or 720p under the Video tab.
    • For music: select 320 kbps under the Audio (MP3) tab.
  7. Click Download. Your browser will immediately download the file to your Downloads folder.

2. Downloading on iPhone & iPad (iOS 13 and later)

  1. Open the YouTube app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share icon below the video and choose Copy Link.
  3. Open Safari and navigate to ytmate.online.
  4. Tap the search bar, tap Paste, and tap Download.
  5. Select your format (e.g. MP3 320k or MP4 1080p). Safari will display a prompt asking: "Do you want to download this file?"
  6. Tap Download. Tap the small download circle in Safari's address bar to access the file in your iOS Files app.

3. Downloading on Android Phones & Tablets

  1. In the YouTube Android app, tap Share > Copy link.
  2. Open Chrome, Firefox, or Samsung Internet and go to ytmate.online.
  3. Paste the link and choose your quality.
  4. Tap Download. The file will save directly into your device's Download folder and appear in your Photos or Music player app.

Pro Tips for Best Quality

  • For Music: Always choose MP3 320 kbps or M4A to retain full dynamic acoustic range.
  • For Big Screen Displays: Choose 4K (2160p) or 1080p (60fps) for buttery-smooth action scenes.
  • For Mobile Storage Conservation: 720p HD or 480p gives great quality while taking up 70% less space.
