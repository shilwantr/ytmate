Yes! YTmate is 100% free with unlimited downloads. You do not need to register, create an account, enter credit card details, or install third-party plugins.

YTmate supports all resolutions that the original YouTube upload provides: including 8K Ultra HD (4320p), 4K (2160p), 2K (1440p), 1080p Full HD (60fps), 720p HD, 480p, 360p, 240p, and 144p.

Absolutely! You can choose the Audio tab to extract MP3 in 320kbps (studio quality), 256kbps, 192kbps, 128kbps, as well as M4A, AAC, and WAV formats.
Can I download YouTube videos on my iPhone or Android device? ▾
Yes. On Android, simply open Chrome or any browser, paste the link, and tap download. On iPhone (iOS 13+), Safari allows direct file downloads into your Files app or Photos library.

Where are the downloaded files saved on my device? ▾
On Windows and Mac, files are saved in your default "Downloads" folder. On Android and iOS, you can find them in your "Downloads" directory or your mobile Files manager app.
