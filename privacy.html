Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

At YTmate, we are deeply committed to user privacy. We believe web utilities should not require surveillance or permanent data gathering.

1. Zero Log Guarantee

We do not store logs of URLs submitted by users, converted files, or download histories on our persistent databases. URLs submitted in the tool are processed ephemerally in RAM strictly to deliver the stream to your browser, after which the buffer is discarded.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers (no account required).
  • We do not store payment or financial details.
  • We do not track files to individual IP addresses.

3. Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) solely to remember your chosen theme preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode). No tracking identifiers are stored.

4. SSL / HTTPS Security

All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is strictly encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
