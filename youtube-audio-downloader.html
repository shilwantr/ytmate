Audio Format Guide: WAV vs M4A vs MP3

Acoustic Engineering Reference

If you're using audio for music production, podcasts, or high-end listening, choosing the correct container format matters:

  • WAV: An uncompressed linear pulse code modulation (LPCM) format. No frequency degradation or lossy psychoacoustic compression. Essential for video editors and music remixers.
  • M4A (AAC): YouTube's original high-tier audio format is AAC. Downloading M4A gives you the pure, untranscoded audio track without any quality loss from re-encoding.
  • MP3: The universal standard. Compatible with all cars, legacy MP3 players, and smartphones.
